NovaTech Industries Ltd. - Annual Report FY2026 (Excerpt)
Chairman's Message:
FY2026 was a landmark year for NovaTech Industries. The company
generated revenue of Rs. 850 crore, up 18% year-on-year, driven by
strong demand in our core manufacturing and digital services
segments. Operating margins improved on the back of cost
discipline and operational efficiency initiatives.
Business Highlights:
- Revenue grew 18% to Rs. 850 crore
- Net profit grew 12% to Rs. 105 crore
- Expanded into two new international markets
- Total debt increased 7% to fund capacity expansion
Risk Factors:
- Foreign exchange volatility
- Rising raw material costs
- Increasing competition in the digital services segment
Outlook:
The management expects continued double-digit revenue growth in
FY2027, supported by new product launches and capacity expansion.
